## What we are building

whishh is a web app for wishlists, gifts and invitations. A short swipe questionnaire builds a taste profile, so a guest who opens a shared link can pick a gift within their budget in under a minute, without signing up. Reservations stay hidden from the list owner, so the surprise survives.

## Privacy boundary

- Guests need no account; their email is optional
- The list owner never sees who reserved what
- No ads, no third-party trackers
